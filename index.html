Information is used to retrieve technology news, generate summaries and social media content, and publish posts when authorized by the account owner.

Account credentials and authentication tokens should be stored securely using the credential management features of n8n. They should not be shared publicly.

6. Data Sharing

Personal information is not intentionally sold. Information may be processed by the connected services described above to operate the workflow.
